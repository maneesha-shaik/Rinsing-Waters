Floods are among the most frequent and destructive natural disasters, causing significant loss of life, property, and
infrastructure every year. Traditional flood prediction techniques rely heavily on physical and statistical models, which 
often struggle to handle large datasets, rapidly changing weather patterns, and complex environmental interactions.

The Rising Waters project addresses these challenges by leveraging machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and 
relationships within hydrological and meteorological data. By analyzing parameters such as rainfall intensity, river water 
levels, soil moisture, and historical flood records, the system can predict flood occurrences more effectively.

This project demonstrates how data-driven approaches can enhance flood forecasting accuracy and provide reliable
early warnings. The system can be used as a decision-support tool for disaster management authorities, smart city applications, 
and environmental monitoring systems.
